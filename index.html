On site, even with no signal

The log is a web page. After the first visit it can be used offline. Times are stamped when you tap Arrive or Depart. Photos are downscaled and stored only on this device.

Fields built from industry guidance

The visit form follows what SIMA describes for in-season documentation: service type, locations, materials and amounts, and arrival and departure times to the minute. Weather notes ask you to name a source.

A season report you can hand over

Print or save a PDF for one property, or download a CSV. Keep a backup from the Operator & backup screen so a phone wipe does not take the winter with it.
