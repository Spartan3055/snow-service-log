About Storm File

Storm File is a small, free website for people who plow, shovel, or salt on the side or as a one-truck business. The centerpiece is an offline service log: no account, no subscription, and no data sent to us. A paid kit of sample forms is sold separately.

The visit fields were written after reading SIMA’s public guidance on effective in-season documentation (retrieved November 19, 2026). That article is SIMA’s, not ours. We are not affiliated with SIMA.

The site is published as a static site. There is no user database. If you clear this browser’s storage, or lose the phone without a backup, the log is gone.

See the disclaimer before you rely on anything here.
