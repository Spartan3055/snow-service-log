The service log

Entries, photos, and times are stored in your browser on your device. We do not receive them. They can be lost if you clear site data, switch browsers, or lose the device. They are not certified, not GPS-verified, and not a complete claims file. Ice and snow can form after you leave a property. A log does not prove that a surface was safe, and it does not transfer liability.

Sample contract and forms

The sample seasonal service agreement and the other kit forms are templates for discussion. They must be reviewed by a licensed attorney in your state before you use them. Laws, insurance requirements, and local ordinances differ. Do not treat a highlighted placeholder as a finished term.

Pricing information

Published consumer price ranges cited on this site come from third-party pages retrieved on November 19, 2026. They are not what you should charge, and they are not a survey of your market. Your costs, travel, snowfall, and competition will differ. The spreadsheet is a calculator, not financial advice.
