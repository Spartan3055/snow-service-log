Snow plow service log template

A usable service log is a standard list of facts you fill in the same way after every stop. Here is the field list used in the free Storm File log, and a paper version if you want a clipboard in the cab.

The digital template (free)

The Storm File service log is a phone page. You add properties, then open a visit. There is no account. After the first load, a service worker keeps the page available offline. Everything stays in this browser until you export it.

Fields on each visit

  • Arrive / Depart — one tap each, stamped to the minute, as SIMA describes for field logs in its in-season documentation guidance.
  • Snow depth (inches) — a measurement or a clearly stated figure. Leave it blank if you did not measure.
  • Service type — plow, shovel or walkways, salt or ice melt, sand, brine, inspection or patrol, or other.
  • Material type and amount — for example rock salt, 40 lb. SIMA asks for type and quantity, not a vague “salted.”
  • Weather notes and weather source — only with a source or a measurement, such as the National Weather Service or an on-site reading.
  • Areas serviced and areas not serviced — including parked cars, a locked gate, or a customer request to skip a section.
  • Reason if you could not service, and any follow-up.
  • Factual notes — actions and observations, not opinions or blame.
  • Photos — stored only on the device, downscaled, up to four per visit.

Each property also holds a name, address, season label, trigger depth, and surfaces included, so the season report has that context at the top.

Export

From a property you can print a season report (use the browser’s Print dialog and “Save as PDF”) or download a CSV of every visit. Operator & backup will save a JSON copy of the whole log, with or without photos.

The paper template

If you want a printed sheet in the truck, the $14 proof-of-service kit includes a salt and visit log as a Word file and a PDF, with the same core columns: date, arrive, depart, depth, services, material, weather source, notes, and initials. You can also use the digital log and keep the paper sheet as a backup.
