What is in the kit

  • Printable salt and visit log — a paper grid for date, arrival, departure, depth, services, material type and amount, weather source, and initials. Word and PDF.
  • Season price-sheet calculator — an Excel workbook that also opens in Google Sheets and LibreOffice. You enter labor, travel, fuel, overhead, materials, and a target margin. It estimates a per-push price and a seasonal price, then stress-tests a light, average, and heavy winter. Example numbers are labeled as examples, not market rates.
  • Incident report form — a factual form for a slip, a vehicle issue, or property damage reported during or after a visit. Word and PDF.
  • Invoice template — per-push lines, seasonal installments, salt, and extras. Word and PDF.
  • Sample seasonal service agreement — a clearly marked sample with placeholders for surfaces, trigger depth, service window, push cap, overages, and payment terms. The first page tells you to have a local attorney review it. Word and PDF.

What it is not

It is not a lawyer, an insurance policy, or a “court-ready” stamp. It does not set your prices or promise that a log will win a dispute. The free log on this site is not inside the download; it stays free on the web.
