Storm File is a free record-keeping site for solo and side-gig snow and ice operators. It is not affiliated with the Snow & Ice Management Association (SIMA), the National Weather Service, or any city or insurer. Logs you keep here stay in your browser. They are not a guarantee of site conditions after a visit, and they are not a substitute for a contract, insurance, or a lawyer.

This log is a memory aid. It is not a certified record, GPS trace, or weather archive. SIMA notes that weather and site conditions should be recorded only with a reliable source or measurement, and that subjective comments do not belong in a service log.
